✦ Meet the Author ✦

Hi, I'm Maidah!

🌸 About Me

Hello, everyone! My name is Maidah, and I'm 9 years old. I have loved creating stories for many years, and writing is one of my favorite ways to use my imagination.

I started writing my stories when I was younger, but I kept them mostly to myself. Now, I've decided to share them because I feel more confident about my ideas and I think it would be wonderful for other people to enter the little worlds I've created.

✏️ Why I Love Writing

I love writing because I can create my own characters, adventures, and little worlds in my own way. But I don't write stories just for fun. I also want every story to have something meaningful to teach.

I love writing about values such as friendship, kindness, courage, honesty, sharing, and caring for others. My hope is that when someone finishes one of my stories, they have not only enjoyed the adventure but have also learned a little something from it.

💌 A Message for My Readers

Welcome to The World of Maidah! I created this little world for readers who love stories, imagination, friendship, adventure, and learning something new along the way.

I hope you enjoy reading my stories as much as I enjoy writing them. Maybe one of my stories will make you smile, give you courage, teach you an important value, or inspire you to create a story of your own.

Thank you for visiting my little world and opening its pages. 🌷

Keep reading, keep imagining, and always remember that even a small story can have a big message.

Maidah 💕
